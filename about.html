About PNG Uni Hub

Empowering PNG's future leaders through accessible higher education information

Our Mission

To provide accessible, accurate, and up-to-date information about all 8 universities in Papua New Guinea, empowering school leavers and continuing students to make informed decisions about their higher education journey.

Our Vision

To become the leading digital gateway for higher education in Papua New Guinea, connecting students with the resources and information they need to build successful futures.

Who We Are

PNG Uni Hub is a free, open-access platform developed to bridge the information gap between students and universities in Papua New Guinea.

  • Comprehensive university profiles
  • Course listings and entry requirements
  • Tuition fee guides
  • Interactive gallery and video tours
  • Direct contact with admissions offices

Core Values

  • Accessibility – Free and open to all
  • Accuracy – Reliable and up-to-date information
  • Integrity – Transparency in all we do
  • Empowerment – Helping students succeed
  • Innovation – Embracing technology for good

Our Partnership

PNG Uni Hub is developed in partnership with the Department of Higher Education, Research, Science and Technology (DHERST) — the government agency responsible for overseeing and coordinating higher education in Papua New Guinea.

Together, we are committed to providing students with the information they need to pursue their educational goals and contribute to the development of PNG.
