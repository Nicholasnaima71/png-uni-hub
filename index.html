The Problem

  • No centralised platform exists for all 8 universities in PNG.

Leads to confusion and missed opportunities.

The Solution

  • PNG Uni Hub – a free, open‑access platform for all 8 universities.
  • Course listings, fees, entry requirements, and contact details.
  • Interactive gallery, videos, and multimedia content.
